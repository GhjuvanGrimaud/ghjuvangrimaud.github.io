# Research

We combine microbiology, bioinformatics, mathematical modelling and machine learning to understand microbial systems across scales.

## Microbiomes and metagenomics

We study the composition, function and dynamics of microbial communities using shotgun metagenomics and genome-resolved approaches. Questions include community structure, strain variation, microbial functions and links between microbiomes and host or environmental phenotypes.

## Machine learning for microbial function

We develop and apply machine-learning methods for biological sequence analysis, with a particular interest in enzyme-function prediction and biologically informed models. The goal is not only prediction, but also methods that can support mechanistic interpretation and metabolic modelling.

## Quantitative microbial ecology

We use mathematical and computational models to investigate microbial interactions, ecological dynamics and evolution. This includes approaches inspired by dynamical systems, metabolic modelling and statistical ecology.

## Applications

Current applications span human microbiomes, microbial biotechnology, food and environmental systems. Projects often combine method development with analysis of large public or collaborative datasets.
