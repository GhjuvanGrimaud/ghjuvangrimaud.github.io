## Research directions

<div class="lab-grid">
  <div class="lab-card">
    <h3>Microbiomes & metagenomics</h3>
    <p>We analyse complex microbial communities using shotgun metagenomics, comparative genomics and ecological approaches.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lab-card">
    <h3>AI for microbial function</h3>
    <p>We develop machine-learning methods to connect biological sequences to enzyme function, metabolism and phenotype.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="lab-card">
    <h3>Quantitative microbial ecology</h3>
    <p>We use mathematical and computational models to study microbial interactions, dynamics and adaptation.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## Our approach

Our work sits at the interface of **microbiology, bioinformatics, systems biology and machine learning**. We are especially interested in methods that remain biologically interpretable and can be applied to real experimental or clinical datasets.
